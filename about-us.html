About us

Aurion.

Aurion Creative is an engineering-led AI and software studio in Pretoria. We build AI systems, AI chatbots and custom software for engineering, construction and property offices, corporate ESD and transformation teams, and established South African businesses that want AI doing real work.

We bring the discipline engineers bring to plant. Work is specified before it is built, tested on real work, commissioned against the specification and monitored after launch. Our AI is engineered with tolerances: confidence thresholds, human review of anything uncertain, and logs of what it did and why.

We also build and run two products of our own, Project Command and BeeLevel. They are how we prove the method in production, and they keep us honest about what it takes to run software every day.

Founded by Thato Kgopa, mechanical engineer (University of Pretoria). Aurion Creative (Pty) Ltd is a Level 1 B-BBEE contributor (EME), with 135% procurement recognition. A B-BBEE affidavit is available on request.

Our method

We commission software the way engineers commission plant.

Specify, Build, Test on real work, Commission, Monitor. The same five stages on every build.

1

Specify

The problem, in numbers: how many documents, how many hours, what error rate you live with now and what good looks like.

2

Build

Against that specification, in stages you can see.

3

Test on real work

Your documents and enquiries, not samples. Real work is where AI holds its tolerances or doesn't.

4

Commission

You sign off against the specification before it goes live.

5

Monitor

We watch it after launch, report against the same numbers and correct what drifts.
