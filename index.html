FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers on what we build, what it costs and how we keep AI honest.

Three things: AI systems and automation (document processing, data extraction, workflow automation and internal AI tools), AI chatbots and assistants for your website and WhatsApp, and custom software and platforms. We also build and run two products of our own, Project Command and BeeLevel.

Both tiers are trained only on your information: your services, prices, policies and FAQs. They are set up never to invent a price or a promise. When a question falls outside what they know, or a customer asks for a person, they hand over. The AI Website Assistant sends the lead and the conversation to your email or a Google Sheet. The AI Enquiry Desk qualifies the lead, books it into your calendar or logs it in your CRM, and reports monthly on how enquiries were handled. After launch we review conversations and correct what needs correcting.

It follows the method. Specify (the Workflow Audit) takes 1 to 2 weeks. Build takes 2 to 6 weeks depending on scope; an AI Enquiry Desk is usually live in 2 to 3 weeks. Test on real work takes 1 to 2 weeks. Commissioning is a sign-off session against the specification. Monitoring continues for as long as the system runs.

Hosting, monitoring and support. We watch the system after launch, fix what breaks, keep its knowledge current and report on what it did. WhatsApp messaging fees from Meta are passed through at cost.

We build with POPIA in mind. Access is limited by role, your data is used only to run your system, and it stays yours. We export or delete it when you ask.
