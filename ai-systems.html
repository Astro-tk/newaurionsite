What we build

Document AI

Systems that read the documents your team handles every day: BOQs, invoices, applications, reports and forms. They extract the fields you need, check them against your rules and put the result where your team works. Anything the system is unsure of is flagged for a person, not guessed.

Workflow automation

The routine steps between systems: capturing, copying, reconciling, chasing and reporting. We automate the steps that follow rules and route the exceptions to the right person, with a record of each step.

Chatbots and assistants

Two tiers, both trained on your own information, both set up never to invent prices or promises, and both monitored after launch. The AI Website Assistant is website chat that captures leads to your email or a Google Sheet and hands over to a person. The AI Enquiry Desk is a complete enquiry system on your website and WhatsApp: CRM integration (HubSpot, Zoho or Pipedrive), appointment booking into your calendar, lead qualification, handover to a person and a monthly performance report.

Internal AI tools

Assistants for your own team: search across your documents, first drafts of routine reports and summaries of long threads, built so your team can check where an answer came from.

How we engineer AI

We treat AI like any other component in a system. It has tolerances, and we design for the cases where it falls outside them.

Tolerances

Every output carries a confidence level. We set the threshold with you, in numbers, while we specify the work.

Human review

Anything below the threshold goes to a person. The system asks before it assumes.

Logs

Every action is recorded: what the AI did, on which input, and why. You can trace a result back to its source.

Monitoring

After launch we watch accuracy, escalations and drift, and correct what needs correcting. The monthly fee pays for this work.

What we don't sell

AI that runs unattended on decisions that matter, or AI meant to take people out of the loop. We build AI that does the routine work and escalates the rest.
