Answers from the source, not from guesswork.

A career guide chatbot

Aug 2025 · AI chatbot

High school learners face long prospectuses and admission requirements that differ by university and programme. We built an AI assistant to turn that material into specific answers.

1. Define the question it must answer

Which subjects and marks does a programme need, and which universities offer it. A narrow, specific job is easier to get right than a general one.

2. Train it on local, current material

We used admission requirements published by South African universities, so answers come from the source rather than from general knowledge.

3. Specific over generic

The assistant answers the learner's actual question with the requirements and options that apply, instead of general study advice.

What we took from it

The same principle runs through every chatbot we build now: a narrow job, trained on the client's own information, with a clear route to a person when the material doesn't cover the question.
