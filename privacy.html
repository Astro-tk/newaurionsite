Privacy

Privacy notice

How Aurion Creative handles the information you give us on this website, in the website chat and on WhatsApp. Last updated 24 Nov 2026.

Who we are

Aurion Creative (Pty) Ltd (Reg. 2026/320259/07), Pretoria, is the responsible party for your personal information under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Our Information Officer is Thato Kgopa. Contact us at info@aurioncreative.com or +27 65 546 5919.

What we collect and why

  • Website chat (Aurion Assistant). The messages you type, so the assistant can answer them. Before it asks for your name, company, email address or phone number, the assistant asks: "May I take your details so Thato can follow up? We use them only to reply to this enquiry." We keep those details only after you say yes. If you type an email address or phone number before saying yes, it is removed automatically and not kept.
  • WhatsApp. Your WhatsApp number, profile name and messages, so we can reply. The same consent question applies before we record your details as an enquiry.
  • Enquiry and contact forms. What you enter, so we can reply.
  • Website analytics. Pages visited and general device information, through Google Analytics, to see how the site is used.

How the assistant works

The website chat and our WhatsApp number are answered by an AI assistant that works only from Aurion's own published information. It can make mistakes, so a person confirms anything that matters before you rely on it. When you have agreed to share your details, the assistant also rates how well your enquiry fits our services, so we can reply to the most urgent first. It makes no decision about you that has legal or similar effects; a person reads every enquiry.

Who processes your information for us

We use these service providers. Each handles your information only to provide its service to us.

  • Anthropic (United States) generates the assistant's AI responses from your chat and WhatsApp messages, and rates enquiries. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, these messages are not used to train its models.
  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform) carries WhatsApp messages between you and us, and sends us a WhatsApp alert about a new enquiry.
  • Google stores the enquiry record (Google Sheets) when you have agreed to share your details, and provides website analytics (Google Analytics).
  • Vercel runs the assistant's server, and Upstash stores chat conversations for 90 days.
  • Resend sends us an email alert about a new enquiry.
  • Formspree receives the website's enquiry and contact forms.
  • GitHub hosts this website.

Information sent outside South Africa

These providers store or process information in the United States, the European Union or elsewhere. We use providers that are bound by agreements giving your information a level of protection comparable to POPIA, as section 72 of POPIA requires.

How long we keep it

  • Chat and WhatsApp conversations: deleted automatically 90 days after the last message.
  • Enquiry records you agreed to: kept while we are in contact about your enquiry, and deleted when you ask us to.
  • Form submissions and emails: kept while we are in contact, and deleted when you ask us to.

What is stored in your browser

The website chat keeps a random ID in your browser's local storage so a conversation can continue, and the conversation itself in session storage until you close the tab. Google Analytics sets its own cookies.

Your rights

You may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, object to how we use it, or withdraw your consent at any time. Email info@aurioncreative.com. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za).

Changes

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this notice and the date at the top.
