The problem

Enterprise and Supplier Development programmes run all year, but the evidence for them is often gathered in the weeks before verification: agreements in inboxes, documents that have expired, payment records in spreadsheets and milestones without proof. The scramble costs time, and gaps found late are hard to close.

What it does

Beneficiary directory

One record for every supported enterprise and supplier, with its documents, disbursements and milestones.

Document vault

Supporting documents stored against each beneficiary, with warnings at 60, 30 and 7 days before a document expires.

Disbursement ledger

An append-only record of every disbursement. Entries are never edited or deleted, and each one needs two-person approval.

Milestones with evidence

Programme milestones tracked with the evidence that shows they were met.

Document and Evidence Readiness score

A measure of how complete your documentation is. It measures documentation completeness only.

Verification Pack

One click produces a ZIP of your programme records and evidence, with a SHA-256 checksum for every file so its integrity can be checked.

What it does not do

“Verifiers decide the points. You bring the proof.”

BeeLevel does not calculate B-BBEE points or levels, and it does not certify anything. It keeps your programme records and evidence complete, organised and ready to hand over. It is built with POPIA in mind.

Who it's for

Corporate Enterprise and Supplier Development and transformation teams that want their programme records ready for verification at any point in the year, not only in the weeks before it.
