Scope

AI chatbot

Audience

High school learners

Knowledge

South African university admission material

Year

The problem

University prospectuses are long, and admission requirements differ by university and programme. High school learners need specific answers: which subjects and marks a programme asks for, and which universities offer it.

What we built

An AI assistant trained on current admission requirements published by South African universities.

It answers a learner's specific question with the subject requirements and university options that apply, instead of general study advice.
