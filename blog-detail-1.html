Structure before style.

Revamping the GLC website

Nov 2025 · Client work

Global Leadership Consultants offer services, courses and funding support. The revamp had one job: make that range easy to navigate, and make it easy to reach a person.

1. Start from what visitors need to find

We listed every offer and grouped them into three pillars: Services, Courses and Funding. Navigation follows those pillars, so a visitor reaches the right page in a click or two.

2. Make the route to a person obvious

For a consultancy, the website's job is to start a conversation. Chat with a team member is a clear, consistent route on the site.

3. Design for a corporate audience

A restrained visual language, professional imagery and a consistent colour palette, so the site matches the firm's reputation.
